No code generation tool can ever surpass the need for understanding, how things work. No matter how sophosticated they are, they are limited by the patterns they are trained on. If you wish to be not limited by conventions already layed out and like to think for approaches never thought before - you may find my writing helpful. I see a great joy in sharing what I learnt the hard way - the easy way.

My name is Prashant Sharma, currently working at IBM India software labs, as a senior software engineer working on Prestodb in OSS and internally. Previously, worked on [Kubeflow](https://github.com/kubeflow/kubeflow), [Tekton](https://tekton.dev/) and [Apache Spark](https://spark.apache.org/)
